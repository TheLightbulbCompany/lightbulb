Annual "saves 20%". "All features included", "Unlimited jobs, documents, and files", "Unlimited customer and vendor portal users" (free). "No contract & no setup fees." No free trial: "We don't offer a free trial because we assign every client a Customer Success Manager." 30‑day money‑back on monthly only. [P] https://www.jobtread.com/pricing (2026-10-27). Software Connect puts annual at $159/mo + $16/user [S] https://softwareconnect.com/reviews/jobtread/ (reviews dated 2022–2023).

**Modules (vendor's own grouping).** [P] https://www.jobtread.com/features (2026-10-27)

## 2. What a GC does in it day to day

Five flows, from the vendor's feature pages [P] plus review quotes [S]. Data each touches uses the API object names above.

1. **Estimate → budget → signed contract.** Build from Cost Catalog/templates; estimate lines *are* budget lines (cost_items grouped by cost_codes/cost_types/cost_groups); e‑sign turns the proposal into a contract. Objects: jobs, accounts (customer), cost_items, documents(estimate). [P] features, budgeting. Review: "Very labor intensive to set up" (Bill, 2022‑08‑30) [S] Software Connect.
2. **Buy and cost the job.** Bid requests → POs/work orders to subs; vendor bills compared to POs; time entries from the field; budget "job costing" view with colored depletion. Objects: documents(order, bill), time_entries, cost_items. [P] features, job‑costing. Review: "track job costs IN REAL TIME, manage jobs, assign tasks to team members, schedule with gantt charts" (Mauricio V., consultant, 1–25 staff, 2023‑07‑19) [S] https://sourceforge.net/software/product/JobTread/ ; "I mainly use Jobtread for job costing/project estimating/budgeting" (G2, via search snippet) [U].
3. **Change orders.** "Simply select the items you want included on your Change Orders"; customer approves via secure link, "totals are adjusted in real time", approvals "automatically tracked back to the budget". Objects: documents(change order), cost_items. [P] https://www.jobtread.com/features/change-orders
4. **Invoice and collect.** "Turn your estimates into invoices with just a few clicks"; "Customize payment schedules so you can bill your customers as the job progresses"; invoice "data automatically syncs with QuickBooks". Online payments via Stripe: cards 2.95% + $0.30, ACH 1% + $1 capped at $15 (page examples: $2,000 invoice → $59.30 card / $15 ACH). Objects: documents(invoice), document_payments. [P] https://www.jobtread.com/features/invoicing , https://www.jobtread.com/features/collect-payments. Review: "Integration with QuickBooks is not always smooth" [S] Software Connect.
5. **Run the schedule and the customer.** Tasks with due dates, daily logs/photos, time tracking; customer portal shows "the full schedule, the different phases, or just what you've assigned to them", shared files/photos, "status and change history for all project documents… estimates, change orders, and invoices", comments with notifications, pay from the portal. Objects: tasks, daily_logs, files, comments. [P] https://www.jobtread.com/features/customer-portals. Review: portal "reduce[s] email inbox" [S] SourceForge.

Common complaints in aggregated reviews (search snippets, [U]): learning curve and setup effort, keeping up with frequent feature changes, QuickBooks sync "disappointing", every estimate must start as a numbered Job, customer search slow with hundreds of customers in kanban.

## 3. Where a non‑JobTread contractor's data lives today

**QuickBooks Online (the one API that matters).**
- Jobs are sub‑customers: Customer entity with `Job` (boolean), `ParentRef` (required on create for a sub‑customer), `BillWithParent`. [S] CData/field docs via search, e.g. https://cdn.cdata.com/help/RNM/odbc/pg_table-customers.htm. QBO "Projects" (Plus/Advanced/Enterprise Suite) are sub‑customers "under the hood" with bill‑parent on. [S] https://billergenie.helpjuice.com/customers/parentsub-customer. Feature flag: `Preferences.OtherPrefs.ProjectsEnabled`. [P snippet, page JS, U] https://developer.intuit.com/app/developer/qbo/docs/workflows/manage-projects
- Project API is GraphQL, scope `project-management.project`, and Intuit's own page calls it "a premium API for Silver, Gold, and Platinum partners" [P snippet, U] https://developer.intuit.com/app/developer/qbo/docs/workflows/manage-projects/get-started. Fields from Intuit's sample app: `projectManagementProjects` list with id, name, description, type, status, startDate, dueDate, completedDate, assignee, priority, customer, account, addresses; update takes `completionRate`. [P] https://github.com/IntuitDeveloper/Sampleapp-Project-Nodejs (2026-10-27). So a small shop building for one contractor should plan on sub‑customers, not the Project API.
- Transaction entities relevant to job cost: Customer, Vendor, Estimate, Invoice, Payment, Bill, BillPayment, Purchase (expenses), Class, Item, TimeActivity — exactly the set JobTread's own sync diagram maps [P] integrations/quickbooks-online.
- Reports API: there is no "JobProfitability" endpoint in QBO (that is a Desktop report). The equivalent is `GET /v3/company/{realmId}/reports/ProfitAndLoss?customer=<ids>&summarize_column_by=Customers` (allowed values include Total, Month, Customers, Vendors, Classes, ProductsAndServices), plus `ProfitAndLossDetail?customer=` and `CustomerIncome`. [S] https://www.withone.ai/knowledge/quickbooks/conn_mod_def%3A%3AGJ6MkXEPQnA%3A%3AW8hWD2X2TKqbZ34b0G9Q4w/md ; [P snippet, U] https://developer.intuit.com/app/developer/qbo/docs/workflows/run-reports. Caveat: P&L by customer only shows what the bookkeeper tagged to the sub‑customer; untagged bills vanish from "job cost".
- Rate limits: 500 requests/min per realm; 10 req/s per realm per app; 40 batch requests/min; ~10 concurrent; HTTP 429 `ThrottleExceeded`. [P snippet, page JS, U] https://developer.intuit.com/app/developer/qbo/docs/learn/limits-and-throttles ; [S] https://coefficient.io/quickbooks-api/quickbooks-api-rate-limits (2025-08-17).
- Program status: "Intuit reviews every app that connects to a production QuickBooks Online… company, whether or not the app is listed on the QuickBooks App Store"; technical/security/marketing review; annual security re‑review; compliance issues "fixed within 2 weeks". [P snippets, pages JS, U] https://developer.intuit.com/app/developer/qbo/docs/go-live/list-on-the-app-store. Reading: a one‑off tool on the contractor's own developer account avoids this; anything distributed goes through review.
- QuickBooks Desktop: not covered by any source fetched; assume Excel/IIF export or the Web Connector SDK [U].

**Buildertrend.** Legacy Budget: "Create an Excel copy by select Export on the top." Job Costing Budget has saved views Standard, Job Costing, Client Pricing, Profit View, populated only after "Send to Budget" from the Estimate; roadmap note says Excel export now "export[s] items without having to manually open each related tag". Pages return 403; all via search snippets. [U] https://buildertrend.com/?p=110280 , https://buildertrend.com/help-article/job-costing-budget-overview/

**Procore.** "Users can export a project's budget to the CSV or PDF file format." CSV "Shows all available rows and columns in the budget view"; "Filtering and grouping settings are not available in this format" (CSV); needs 'Standard' permission on Budget. [P] https://v2.support.procore.com/product-manuals/budget-project/tutorials/export-a-budget (2026-10-27)

**Spreadsheets / email.** Sheets or Excel estimates, bills as PDFs in email, schedule in a calendar. No API; the agent reads files.

## 4. "Something similar" at three sizes

Synthesis (mine), using the facts above.

**(a) One‑screen management dashboard, read‑only, from QuickBooks + spreadsheet.**
- Screens: one page. Per active job (QBO sub‑customer): contract value (Estimate total or Sheet cell), invoiced to date (Invoice), collected (Payment), cost to date (Bill + Purchase + TimeActivity tagged to the sub‑customer), budget (from the Sheet), variance, % billed vs % spent, open AR by customer (CustomerBalance / AgedReceivables), unpaid bills (AgedPayables). Optional: a sales‑pipeline strip from Estimates in Pending status.
- Data: nightly or on‑load pull via QBO OAuth (`com.intuit.quickbooks.accounting` scope) and a Sheets read; everything is a **snapshot** with a "data as of" stamp. Nothing writes back.
- Sign‑in: one QBO OAuth consent by the owner; dashboard behind a single password or the owner's Google login. No roles.
- Buy vs build: nothing to buy. The real work is mapping sub‑customers to Sheet rows. Missing vs JobTread: committed cost (POs), bills‑vs‑PO, change‑order rollup, per‑line cost codes unless the bookkeeper uses Items/Classes consistently.

**(b) Multi‑user job tracker: budget vs actual, schedule, change orders; 2–3 people update it.**
- Screens: Jobs list; Job page with budget lines (cost code, est. cost, committed, actual, variance, price), change‑order list that re‑rolls the budget, task/schedule list, notes/photos; the (a) dashboard on top.
- Data: the tool owns budget, change orders and tasks (its own Postgres/SQLite). Actuals are **live‑ish** from QBO (hourly sync of Bills/Purchases/TimeActivity by sub‑customer, with CDC or `MetaData.LastUpdatedTime` queries); the tool writes **nothing** to QBO in v1, so the books stay the bookkeeper's. Sheet import is one‑time.
- Sign‑in: email magic‑link, three roles (owner, office, field), audit log of who changed a budget line. Mobile‑width pages for the field user.
- Buy vs build: e‑sign for change orders (DocuSign/Dropbox Sign) or record "approved by email" with the PDF; file storage. No payments, no SMS.
- Hard parts: change orders that edit existing lines; cost codes drifting from QBO Items; the first request to push the budget to QBO (then Intuit review and two‑way conflicts arrive).

**(c) Full JobTread‑like suite: customer portal, invoicing, payments.**
- Screens: all of (b) plus estimates/proposals with e‑sign, POs/work orders to subs, bills‑vs‑PO, invoices with payment schedules, a customer portal (schedule, documents, approve change orders, pay), sub/vendor portal, time tracking, daily logs, selections.
- Data: the tool becomes the system of record and must **push** invoices, payments, bills and customers to QBO (two‑way sync, with the conflicts JobTread users already complain about).
- Sign‑in: internal roles plus unlimited external logins (customers, subs) with per‑job scoping; this is where the benchmark's 20% "Authorization Issue" failure mode lives.
- Must buy: payments (Stripe; JobTread's own rates are 2.95%+30¢ / ACH 1%+$1 cap $15), e‑sign, SMS (Twilio), email delivery, accounting sync (a QBO app that passes Intuit's security review, or a unified‑API vendor), file storage.
- JobTread's $199 + $20/user is cheaper than the first month of building (c); the smallest published Claude‑built example is already 20 tables and 47 routes (ProBuildCalc, below).

## 5. The ToS line for learning from JobTread

Terms of Service, Effective Date August 1, 2024. [P] https://www.jobtread.com/terms (2026-10-27). WebFetch truncates quotes; clause text is as returned.

- §2.1 Access Rights: "non-exclusive, non-transferable… revocable right" to use the Service.
- §2.2(c): Customer may not "reverse engineer, copy, modify, adapt, hack, or otherwise attempt to gain unauthorized access to the Service".
- §2.2(d): may not "access the Service or JobTread's Confidential Information to build a competitive product or service".
- §2.2(g): may not use "crawlers, robots, or other automated processes to copy, scrape, or systematically acquire any content" "without the express written consent of JobTread".
- §6.1: "Confidential Information specifically includes, but is not limited to, the Service, Order Form(s)…", excluding information that is public or independently developed.
- §7.1: "Customer owns all right, title, and interest in and to Customer Confidential Information and Customer Data"; JobTread gets a license "to host, copy, access, process, transmit, and display Customer Data".
- §7.2: "JobTread owns and will continue to own all right, title, and interest" in the Service.
- No clause on the API, benchmarking, or data export on termination. §5 says rights end and JobTread may deactivate the account; no return‑of‑data promise.

Plain‑English reading (not legal advice):
- **Fine:** reading the public marketing/feature/pricing pages, public webinars and the YouTube channel, public product‑update titles; reading a contractor's *own* exported data (they own it, §7.1); describing JobTread's feature set in your own words; building a competing tool from your own design.
- **Not fine for anyone who has an account (and risky for anyone who uses one):** logging in to inspect the app bundle, network calls, or Pave schema to copy them (§2.2(c)); "accessing the Service… to build a competitive product" (§2.2(d)) — this clause bites a founder who signs up as a customer to study it; crawling the help center or API explorer with a bot (§2.2(g)); using the AI Connector/MCP as a scraping channel (it "operates using your account permissions").
- The ToS binds "Customer". Someone who never signs up is not party to it, but copyright on JobTread's text/screens and the anti‑scraping clause on the public site still apply, and pages on `app.jobtread.com` are inside the Service.

## 6. What a coding agent can realistically produce today

**Benchmark.** Vibe Code Bench (arXiv 2603.04601, v3 2026‑05‑13): 100 app specs, 964 browser workflows; best of 16 models, GPT‑5.3‑Codex, 61.77 ± 4.71% of workflow steps on the held‑out split; the generation prompt says "You CANNOT interact with the user at any point, you are on your own" (that is the "unattended" condition); up to 5 h per app; Codex $11.91 / 75.8 min, Claude Opus 4.6 $8.69 / 21.3 min. Apps scoring 100% were "only 8.8% of applications produced by the top five models"; 12.8% of top‑five apps scored zero; worst five models had 14.0% fail to start. Error modes on apps that started: Missing Feature 46.7%, Authorization Issue 20.4%, Validation or Policy Block 14.8%, Other 10.2%, UI Rendering/Navigation 6.0%, Data Consistency/Backend Logic 1.9%. Self‑testing during generation correlates with score (r = 0.72). [P] https://arxiv.org/html/2603.04601

**Published construction examples.**
- ProBuildCalc (BlueWave Projects, Anchorage; vendor case study, undated, © 2026): "Claude architected and built the entire platform across multiple sessions", "the sole engineering resource across 9 build sessions"; estimating from photos, receipt capture, job costing and margin, PDF estimates/invoices, calendar + Twilio SMS; Next.js/Prisma/Postgres/FastAPI/Capacitor; "47 API routes and 20 database tables"; built with a Hawaii flooring contractor; no failures reported (vendor‑authored). [S] https://bluewave-projects.pages.dev/case-studies/probuildcalc
- Business Insider, April 2026 (walled; details via search snippets): construction superintendent Pawel Mniszak vibe‑coded "HrdHat", a field risk‑assessment/paperwork tracker for crews of 50–110; "My workers loved it — until I accidentally broke it"; he added Stripe to test monetization, the Stripe integration failed, and he "kept adding features until the app broke". [U] https://www.businessinsider.com/construction-manager-vibe-coded-paperwork-tool-2026-4
- A derivative write‑up (2026‑04‑09) of a construction manager's paperwork tool lists the failures as: missing validation letting corrupted records through, database not fit for the query patterns, incomplete input sanitization, and "no rollback strategy" so documents could not be restored. [S] https://nameocean.net/article/when-ai-assisted-development-goes-wrong-a-construction-managers-cautionary-tale/
- Non‑construction but instructive (r/ClaudeAI, 2026): a Claude‑built job scraper ran "silently" wrong for days: GitHub cron doesn't run on a free private repo, and `git diff --quiet` "silently misses untracked files, so brand-new state files never got committed". [S] https://reddit.sentinel-team.org/posts/1tjr06f/snapshots/2026-05-23T02%3A20%3A04.239191Z
- No Replit/Lovable/Bolt customer story for a contractor dashboard exists that I could find. [U]

**What this means for (a) and (b).**
- (a) is inside today's envelope: one read‑only page over QBO Reports + a Sheet is a handful of workflows and no authorization matrix; the risk is quiet wrong numbers, not crashes.
- (b) is at the edge: multi‑user roles, budget re‑rolls on change orders and an hourly QBO sync are exactly the Missing‑Feature and Authorization buckets (67% of failures combined). Expect the first unattended build to pass roughly 6 in 10 workflows and to need a human (or a second, test‑writing pass) for the rest.

**The two biggest things an agent‑built version gets wrong.**
1. **Accounting semantics, silently.** "Budget vs actual" in JobTread means estimate vs committed (POs) vs billed (bills matched to POs) vs invoiced, per cost code, with change orders rolled in. QBO's P&L‑by‑customer is none of those; it is whatever the bookkeeper tagged, on cash or accrual. An agent will produce a confident dashboard that is off by every untagged bill and every open PO, and nobody will see an error.
2. **Auth, sync and the long tail of production.** Multi‑role permissions, OAuth token refresh, 429 back‑off, webhooks, rollback/backups and input validation are the pieces the benchmark and the construction anecdotes all name; they are also the pieces a non‑engineer founder cannot check by clicking around.
