An AI-native company operating system is not just a chatbot attached to a task list. It is a workspace where agents can understand goals, break work into projects and tasks, use company context, connect to tools, ask for human approval, and keep working after the approval arrives.

[Lightbulb](/) is built around that model: a private AI workspace, a roster of agents, Paperclip for company operations, and a persistent company brain.

## The core jobs an AI-native company OS should handle

1. **Capture intent as goals.** A founder should be able to say what outcome matters, not micromanage every click.
2. **Turn goals into projects and tasks.** Work needs owners, evidence, status, and a definition of done.
3. **Give agents memory and files.** A useful agent needs durable context, not a blank session every morning.
4. **Connect the outside world.** Inbox, chat channels, product webhooks, ads accounts, repositories, docs, and browser-only tools all need a route into the workspace.
5. **Gate risky actions.** Deployments, spend, refunds, legal terms, customer-facing messages, and destructive changes need human-in-the-loop approval.
6. **Automate recurring work.** Reporting, monitoring, lead follow-up, support triage, content ops, and research should run on schedules or external events.

## How Lightbulb maps those jobs

- **Paperclip** is the operating layer for goals, projects, tasks, owners, comments, evidence, and approvals.
- **The company brain** gives agents shared memory and durable workspace context.
- **Connectors and channels** let agents work where the business already works.
- **Webhooks** turn product or tool events into agent wakeups.
- **The Browser panel** gives agents a practical path for services that do not expose a native connector.
- **The marketplace** lets owners hire specialist agents or deploy prebuilt companies.
- **Approval cards** keep humans in control of governed actions.

## Why this matters for founders

The highest-leverage use of AI at a company is not one-off prompting. It is an operational loop: a goal becomes a task, a task is assigned to an agent, the agent uses the right tools, the agent asks for approval when needed, and the result is recorded for the next loop.

That is the difference between "AI assistance" and an AI-native company.

## Start with one loop

Pick one recurring business workflow:

- inbound lead triage,
- support inbox routing,
- weekly growth reporting,
- GitHub issue triage,
- Meta Ads and Instagram content operations,
- competitor research,
- or webhook-based customer onboarding.

Then give the loop an owner, a trigger, connected tools, and a human approval gate for anything sensitive.
