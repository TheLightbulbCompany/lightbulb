## Refuted or unverifiable

- A4 (R, partial): `Resource` has no phone and no user id; `detail` is untyped. Sender id must come from `POST /users` (handle = line phone) or the first inbound `receivedBy.id`.
- A5 (R, partial): Beta webhook delivery signing is undocumented; the `webhook-id/timestamp/signature` headers are unconfirmed. The docs under `/docs/webhooks/*` are Stable Spectrum (`X-Spectrum-*`, `v0=`), not 2.0. Only `whsec_` secret, Idempotency-Key and the one-time secret are confirmed. Event-types list needs auth.
- A6 (partial): no `direction` or service field; use `status:"received"`, `receivedBy`/`addressedTo`.
- A10 (U): no sandbox documented anywhere (email "simulator traffic" hint only).
- A11 (U): same-number SMS fallback and 10DLC requirement not stated by any source.
- C1 bug: `routers/billing.py:441` passes `plan="standard"`, which `create_workspace_checkout` rejects with `plan_retired`.
- C2 (R, partial): the handler does not read `event_data["id"]` directly and does not touch `text_lines` (the latter confirmed).
- C3 (R): `put_message` keeps only `message-id`, `references`, `in-reply-to` in `headers`.
- C7 (R, partial): predicate compares plan and return_to only (not seats), and ignores front_desk.
