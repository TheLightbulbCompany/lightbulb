Support Inbox is a one-agent operating loop, not a chatbot. You forward a
customer email; the Support Triage agent reads it, sorts it (answer now /
needs you / noise), and sends you back a drafted reply in your voice. You
approve, tweak, or take over. Follow-ups on the same email thread reach the
same conversation, so the agent keeps context across a whole exchange.

How to activate it:

1. Hire this company from the marketplace.
2. Say hello to Support Triage in your Teams inbox. It mints its own support
   address on your company's domain and emails you from it, so you have the
   address in your inbox where you'll actually use it.
3. Forward a customer email from your own mailbox to that address. That
   forward is the whole integration — there is nothing else to configure.

The agent never emails your customers directly. Replies go back to you, and
you send the final word. That is a rule the agent follows and a boundary the
platform enforces.
