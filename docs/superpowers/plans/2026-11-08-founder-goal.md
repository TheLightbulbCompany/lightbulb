**The goal.** A business signs up, names its assistant, forwards its phone, and within 15 minutes the assistant answers calls, books and moves appointments in the business's own system, takes messages, and puts anything it cannot decide in front of the owner. The owner sees every call and meeting and exactly what the assistant did, and steers it by talking to it. **Architecture you must keep.** The super-agent architecture already built: a Router and per-task Hermes Workers on Temporal, tasks, questions, approval modes, Knowledge, memory. Workers are Hermes sessions per task or per meeting on a pooled AgentCore runtime; no VM per business, no secret per business, no always-on process per business.
