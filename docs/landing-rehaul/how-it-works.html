how it works

You hire the team. They do the work.

isol8 gives you a private cloud company. You describe what needs doing; Friday works out who should do it and hands the job over. You stay in the loop only where it counts.

01

Hire from the marketplace

Every listing is a working agent, not a template. Stitch does sales outreach, Tally keeps the books, Ora guards your calendar, Lens does research. Hiring takes one click — the agent lands in your container already knowing its job, its tools, and the shape of its work.

02

Connect the tools you already use

Gmail, Slack, Discord, Telegram, WhatsApp, your CRM, your calendar. Agents work inside the accounts you already have rather than asking you to move into a new app. Message them the way you'd message a coworker.

03

Friday coordinates the rest

Friday is your orchestrator. She turns a goal into a set of jobs, hands each one to whoever owns it, chases what's outstanding, and reports back. As the company grows, Atlas finds or designs the next specialist to fill the gap.

04

You approve anything risky

Agents run on their own, but stop and ask before anything with consequences — sending to a customer, moving money, shipping a change. Everything runs in your own isolated container: no other tenant can reach your files or conversations, and your data is never used to train models.
