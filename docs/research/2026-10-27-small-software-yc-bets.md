## 2. YC Requests for Startups text that touches the thesis

Caveat on provenance: on 2026-10-27, [ycombinator.com/rfs](https://www.ycombinator.com/rfs) renders only the **Winter 2027** edition (11 requests) [P]; older editions are in the nav but their bodies do not load. Guessed per-edition URLs 404. The Summer 2026, Fall 2026 and Spring 2026 texts below come from Modelence's mirror, which explicitly rewrites each request as an "AI-ready prompt" ("Build a production-ready...") — treat wording as **[S] paraphrase**, not YC's prose. No edition uses the phrases "software builds itself", "end of SaaS", "personal software" or "disposable software".

**Summer 2026** (published "late April" 2026 [S](https://ccstartup.com/blog/2026/05/02/ycs-summer-2026-rfs-bets-on-agriculture-robots-drone-defence-and-lunar-manufacturing-as-software-loses-its-moat/); opening line quoted as "AI has stopped being a feature and started being the foundation." [S](https://urbangeekz.com/2026/05/y-combinator-reveals-15-startup-ideas-it-wants-founders-to-build-in-summer-2026/) 2026-05-19)
- **Dynamic Software Interfaces** (Ankit Gupta per mirror; press names no partner): "Build a production-ready platform for creating software interfaces that adapt in real time to each user's context" — expertise level, task, behaviour; MVP = novice/expert modes, frustration-triggered simplification [S](https://modelence.com/yc-rfs-summer-2026/dynamic-software-interfaces). Press gloss: "software interfaces users can heavily customize using coding agents."
- **SaaS Challengers** (Jared Friedman): "Create an AI-first challenger to CRM"; "No professional services or implementation partner required"; "One-click migration import from incumbent (Salesforce, HubSpot, Asana, etc.)" [S](https://modelence.com/yc-rfs-summer-2026/saas-challengers). Press: targets ERP, chip-design, industrial-control and supply-chain incumbents [S](https://ccstartup.com/blog/2026/05/02/ycs-summer-2026-rfs-bets-on-agriculture-robots-drone-defence-and-lunar-manufacturing-as-software-loses-its-moat/).
- **AI-Native Service Companies** (Gustaf Alströmer): "deliver a professional service... using AI agents as the primary workforce, with humans in oversight roles only"; "dramatically undercutting traditional service providers on price" [S](https://modelence.com/yc-rfs-summer-2026/ai-native-service-companies).
- Also in the edition: **The AI Operating System for Companies** (Diana Hu), **Company Brain** (Tom Blomfield), **Software for Agents** (Aaron Epstein) [S](https://modelence.com/yc-rfs-summer-2026).

**Fall 2026** — **A Cloud for Small Software** (Pete Koomen): "Traditional clouds (AWS, Azure, GCP) are built for large engineering orgs" and "are far too complex for a single person shipping a small internal tool"; "a platform where an agent-built app goes from code to a shared, secured URL in one step, with deployment, sharing, customization, authentication, and permissions handled for you"; "the anti-AWS-console" [S](https://modelence.com/yc-rfs-fall-2026/a-cloud-for-small-software). Press paraphrase: "sharing a piece of internal software with a colleague is as simple as sharing a Google Doc" [S](https://foundedceo.substack.com/p/y-combinator-just-revealed-13-startup) 2026-09-19. This is the single RFS closest to "software for one".

**Spring 2026** — **AI-Native Agencies** (Eric Migicovsky): "infrastructure for building AI-native service companies with software-like margins"; "Replace traditional agency labor with AI-powered production systems"; scoped to brand/creative design, "not a CRM for agencies" [S](https://modelence.com/yc-rfs-spring-2026/ai-native-agencies). Edition date not shown.

**Winter 2027** (live, [P](https://www.ycombinator.com/rfs)): nothing on this thesis. Nearest: "The $15-an-Hour Expert" (Brad Flora: "AI that turns an ordinary worker into an expert"), "Own Your Intelligence" (Jon Xu).
