# Routing v2 router (rollout step 2): 3×3 multi-party gate

Gate = the MEAN of 3 full runs each (356 cases, live Jev, ZDR, stub cards with role tools), mean (min–max).
- **Baseline**: origin/main `35f8ae86e` + the #3596 bench (`test/multiparty-routing-fix`), with its voice variant made
  Live-faithful the same way as the candidate's: the voice history is parsed from a "Name: text" transcript by main's
  own `voice_calls._transcript_history` (no timestamps, no ids — what `route_live_line` sees), and a lone speaker's
  "none" takes the fallback (main's `humans > 1` rule). This is why baseline numbers differ slightly from [MP].
- **Candidate**: `feat/routing-v2-router`; the bench calls the production `messaging.address`,
  `select_dispatch_targets`, `jev.speaks_to_people`, `jev._decide_for_message` and `voice_calls._transcript_history`.
  Voice is scored as the SHIPPED worker delivers it (`--voice-worker shipped`, the default): `/route`'s `agentId`
  alone, and `hold` ignored. Chat: 3 runs (`cand-*`); voice: 3 runs after the Codex fixes (`candv-*`).
- Aggregator: `_agg.py` (this directory). Acceptance = §12.5 on means (cued recall ≥ 0.9, precision ≥ 0.95) AND no class
  mean worse than the baseline mean by more than 0.03 (recall, false-speak or precision). **Result: no class worse than baseline; one §12.5 miss (voice precision 0.94, below).**

## Diagnosis of the earlier F / B13 regression
Ablation, mean P(expected) over 3 reps per row (chat/voice), against the candidate before the fix:
removing the WHO proxy-request sentence alone brought B13 (0.29 → 0.35, 0/3 → 3/3) and F06 (0.46 → 0.55, 0/3 → 3/3)
back to the old-WHO level; restoring the old line format, the old NONE_CRITERION, removing `self` or the
reask/question questions did not. Two rewordings of that sentence ("treated as if that person had asked",
"still a request to the room") did not beat removing it, and removing it costs nothing on A (on-behalf-of) rows: the
addressing stage's `relayed` label already routes named proxies (C 1.00). So the sentence is dropped. `state.open` is
empty in the bench (no open runs), so it was not a factor.
